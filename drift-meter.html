Being rebuilt

The instrument is not here yet. The essays it was built to accompany are.

Versions v0.1 to v0.5 of the Drift Meter shipped the instrument as a single generated file of about 894 KB, with no editable source anywhere on disk, no tests, and no continuous integration. It worked. It was also impossible to correct with any confidence, which for an instrument whose entire claim is that it measures something is the wrong property to have.

So it is being rebuilt from source, one reviewable change at a time. The case data, the cost model and the derived measures are done and under test; the screens are not. Rather than leave a link here that goes nowhere, this page says so.

The rebuild is tracked in the open, including the parts that are not finished: the issues list what is left, and the changelog records what changed in each version and why, retractions included.

In the meantime, the argument the instrument exists to make is written down in full.
